A circuit does not have to be complicated. A handful of bodyweight movements, a rep target for each, and a timed rest between them is enough for a focused session. Voice Rep Counter lets you build that structure out of individual rep and rest sessions.

## Start with a simple structure

A basic three-exercise circuit might look like this:

- Push-ups — 10 repetitions
- Squats — 12 repetitions
- Plank — 45 seconds

After each exercise, add a short rest. The rest can be the same length throughout, or shorter or longer depending on the movement.

## Build the sessions

Create one **Reps Counter** session per timed exercise:

1. `Push-ups` — 10 reps, 3 seconds between numbers.
2. `Squats` — 12 reps, 4 seconds between numbers.

Then create a **Rest Timer** session for your plank hold and your rest periods:

3. `Plank` — a 45-second rest timer.
4. `Rest` — a 30-second rest timer between exercises.

## Put them in order

Once the sessions exist, reorder them into the circuit flow. For example:

`Push-ups → Rest → Squats → Rest → Plank → Rest`

With optional **sequential playback**, the app can advance through the configured order, and **Play All** runs the sessions you have set up. You can pause and resume at any point.

## Why this works

Each session is saved locally, so you can reuse the circuit next time without rebuilding it. Because the pacing is spoken, you spend less time checking the screen and more time moving.

## What to keep in mind

Voice Rep Counter paces by time and spoken counts. It does not detect completed reps or evaluate form. If an exercise does not feel right, stop, adjust, and resume when you are ready.
